Janine's Boblog

A blog of my boba and matcha adventures



Fat Milk

Ube Matcha - July 26, 2026

Went to Argyle for the first time to try some of the best pho and banh xeo spots. After a quick lunch at Pho 888, I went down the street to try Fat Milk. The Ube Matcha with 0% sugar and light ice had some of the best ube and matcha flavors I've had at a coffee location, giving it a solid 10/10 for the true ube flavor it held. (Photo is definitely not the best, I drank most of it before forgetting to take a picture)



HeyTea

Coconut Mango Boom - July 24, 2026

After work, I explored Chinatown and tried some sweet treats from Chiu Quon Bakery (I love their sesame cookies) and stopped by HeyTea to try some new drinks. Coconut Mango Boom 0% sugar after a long work and gym day... solid 9.8 out of 10. I love HeyTea :D



Molly Tea

Matcha Jasmine Salted Cheese - July 3, 2026

After work, we decided to peruse Chinatown and check out some of my favorite matcha spots. Today's matcha was a solid 9/10, definitely hit the spot after a long and rainy work day.



Cafe Blessing

Blueberry Matcha - July 24, 2026

After opening in Schaumburg, I tried their matcha and scones in the Chicagoland area. Their matcha wasn't the greatest, so I'd give it a 7/10 for their lack of nuttiness and richness in matcha quality. However, their biscoff and cookies and cream flavored scones were PHENOMENAL. 10/10 recommend any of their scone flavors, especially the cream ones.
