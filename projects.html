Mandated Reporter Resources App

The Miseducation of Mandated Reporters

Persuasion 2026-2027

While competiing on the UIC Forensics team, my junior year persuasion (a speech dedicated on educating others about a problem, and ways the audience can help fix it) is about the miseducation of mandated reporters across the United States. With inconsistent laws and regulations surrounding this issue, it forces millions of children and families across the United States to enter the CPS system, despite the report being unsubstantiated. In this app, it created a centralized resource where mandated reporters can be provided with alternatives to report any concerns that they have.

PalmWatch

The Palm Oil Crisis:

A Forecast of Deforestation in Sumatra

In collaboration with the University of Chicago's Data Science Institute and the human rights organization Inclusive Development International (IDI), they created PalmWatch, a tool to retrospectively analyze any environmental harm due to palm oil. In this project, we created machine learning models to forecast future deforestation across the island of Sumatra, Indonesia.
