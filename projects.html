PalmWatch

The Palm Oil Crisis:

A Forecast of Deforestation in Sumatra

In collaboration with the University of Chicago's Data Science Institute and the human rights organization Inclusive Development International (IDI), they created PalmWatch, a tool to retrospectively analyze any environmental harm due to palm oil. In this project, we created machine learning models to forecast future deforestation across the island of Sumatra, Indonesia.
